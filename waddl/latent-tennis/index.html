LATENT trains athletic tennis motion from imperfect human demonstrations, then uses latent-action control and high-level reinforcement learning to turn single strokes into continuous play.

The published repository includes the Unitree G1 MuJoCo model, racket attachment, scene files, tracking pipeline and sample motions. Those redistributable model files are mirrored here with their original license and provenance.

Honest boundary: this page is a real-time browser visual twin driven by a deterministic rally state machine. It is not the unreleased LATENT high-level policy running in MuJoCo. The official repository still lists that policy, its pretrained latent-action model and sim-to-real design as future releases.
